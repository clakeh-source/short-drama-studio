THE LAST FERRY
Episode 1 — "Manifest"

INT. HARBOUR TERMINAL - NIGHT

Rain sheets against the glass. MEI LIN, 32, stands at the closed
ticket window with a duffel bag she has not put down since she
arrived. The departures board flips over to a single word: DELAYED.

She takes out a phone. One bar. She dials anyway.

MEI
He's not on the manifest. I checked it twice.

A pause. Somewhere behind her a metal shutter comes down.

MEI (CONT'D)
Then whoever boarded is using his name.

INT. FERRY DECK - NIGHT

The engines shudder awake. DANIEL VOSS, 40s, expensive coat, wrong
shoes for weather, watches the terminal recede. He is holding a
boarding pass that is not his.

Mei steps out of the stairwell behind him. He does not turn around.

DANIEL
You should have stayed on the dock.

She sets the duffel down. For the first time all night, she lets
go of it.

MEI
And you should have used a different name.
